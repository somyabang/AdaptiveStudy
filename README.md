****Your Adaptive Study Companion 📚🤖****
Plan Better. Focus Longer. Learn Smarter.

Your Adaptive Study Companion is a student-focused web app that makes studying more organized and productive. It brings study planning, focus sessions, reminders, AI help and progress tracking together in one place.

***💡 Inspiration***

Students often struggle with distractions, time management and staying consistent. We wanted to build a digital companion that helps them plan their study time, stay focused and keep making progress.

***✨ Features***

📅 Smart Study Planner: add tasks with deadlines and importance, and get a plan that fits your available time.

🔄 Adaptive Replanning: the plan updates when time changes or a session is missed, and explains why.

⏱️ Study Timer and Pomodoro: track study and break time, with subject-wise totals.

🔔 Focus Guardian: reminders with Start, Snooze and Dismiss, plus an "I'm distracted" button.

🤖 AI Doubt Solver, Quiz and Weak Topic Finder (needs AI connection, see below).

📝 Flashcards, Notes Q&A and Study Journal for revision.

⭐ XP, Streaks and Badges to make studying more engaging.

📈 Progress Dashboard with daily, weekly and monthly study time.

⏳ Exam Countdown to prioritise what to study first.

***🚀 Getting Started***

Download or clone this repository.

Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.

Open index.html in your browser.

Click Demo data to see the app with sample data, or add your own tasks.

For the best experience, use a modern web browser.

**AI features**

Everything works without setup except the AI features. To use them, open the AI Tutor tab, find Connect AI, and add a Worker URL or an API key. Never put an API key inside index.html or upload it to GitHub.
